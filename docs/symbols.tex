\section*{Symbols and conventions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Symbols and conventions}
Units: energies in eV unless a frequency is stated (MHz, GHz) or a wavenumber (\si{\per\centi\metre}); $\hbar=1$ inside the matrices; magnetic field $B$ in tesla (1 T $=10^4$ G).
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{p{2.6cm}p{11cm}}
\toprule
Symbol & Meaning\\
\midrule
$H,\ H_0$ & Hamiltonian; the part without spin-orbit or field (a constant energy shift of the level)\\
$\vb L,\ \vb S,\ \vb J$ & orbital, spin and total electronic angular momentum, $\vb J=\vb L+\vb S$\\
$\vb I,\ \vb F$ & nuclear spin and total angular momentum, $\vb F=\vb J+\vb I$\\
$l,\ s,\ j,\ i,\ f$ & quantum numbers of $\vb L,\vb S,\vb J,\vb I,\vb F$\\
$m_l,\ m_s,\ m_j,\ m_I$ & projections on the field axis ($z$); $M=m_l+m_s$ is exactly conserved\\
$\zeta$ & spin-orbit constant in $H_\text{SO}=\zeta\,\vb L\cdot\vb S$ (eV)\\
$\mu_B$ & Bohr magneton; $\mu_B B$ is the Zeeman energy scale (about $5.788\times10^{-5}$ eV/T)\\
$g_l,\ g_s,\ g_J,\ g_I$ & orbital, electron-spin, Land\'e (total) and nuclear g-factors ($g_I$ in units of $\mu_B$, so it is tiny)\\
$A_J$ & hyperfine constant of fine-structure level $J$ in $A_J\,\vb I\cdot\vb J$ (MHz)\\
$B^*=\zeta/\mu_B$ & field at which Zeeman and spin-orbit energies are equal; Paschen--Back sets in well above it\\
$\Delta$ & exchange splitting between singlet and triplet (calcium)\\
$\varepsilon^2$ & probability of singlet character in a level that is mostly triplet\\
$q,\ \pi,\ \sigma^\pm$ & change of $m$ in a transition ($0,\pm1$) and the matching light polarisation. $\sigma^\pm$ here is a polarisation, not an uncertainty\\
$\sigma$ (in the uncertainty section) & standard deviation of a number, and $z=|a-b|/\sqrt{\sigma_a^2+\sigma_b^2}$ the distance between two numbers in standard deviations\\
$A_{ki}$ & Einstein coefficient (decay rate, s$^{-1}$) of a transition; unrelated to $A_J$\\
$E,\ \vb E$ & electric field (V/cm) in the Stark section\\
$e,\ a_0$ & elementary charge, Bohr radius; $e a_0 E$ is the Stark energy scale\\
$\alpha$ & fine-structure constant (about $1/137$)\\
$\mathcal F,\ d,\ R$ & Fabry--P\'erot finesse, mirror gap, and the resolving power $\lambda/\Delta\lambda$ of an instrument\\
$\lambda,\ \tilde\nu$ & wavelength and wavenumber ($1/\lambda$)\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Term symbols: $^{2S+1}L_J$, for example $^2P_{3/2}$ or $3P_{1/2}$ with the principal quantum number in front; $\ket{l,m_l}\otimes\ket{s,m_s}$ is the product (uncoupled) basis used for every matrix.
